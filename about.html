Who we are

About CuePointe

An organised stage for Kampala’s pool players, with every match on the record.

Where it started

Pool has long been a favourite pastime for young people in Kampala. But it stayed informal: few organised competitions, no records, and no way for a good player to be noticed beyond their own neighbourhood.

CuePointe started in Kabalagala, Kansanga and along Ggaba Road with one idea: every player deserves a fair, organised stage.

We run knockouts and leagues, record every match, and keep a public rating for every player, so progress is earned at the table and visible to everyone.
