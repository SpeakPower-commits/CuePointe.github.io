How a knockout night works

  1. 1

    Pay the entry

    Send UGX 10,000 by mobile money to 0743 482 588 (Thomas Otieno). Reference: your name + “Tournament”.

  2. 2

    Register online

    Fill in the registration form and attach the payment screenshot. We confirm your slot on WhatsApp.

    Play at the Meeting Point

    Come to The New Meeting Point, Kataba, Kabalagala before the start time on the schedule.

    Earn your rating

    Every match is recorded. Your rating, wins and events update on the rankings, and you get a CuePointe player profile.
